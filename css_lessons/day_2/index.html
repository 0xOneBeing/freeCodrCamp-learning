This is a post about my favourite food. My favourite food is rice.

I love eating rice so much that I could eat it on and on every time of the day.

I am able eat any type of rice: be it fried rice, jollof rice. coconut rice, chinese rice, basmatik rice, ofade (local) rice, or just plain white rice.

If you want to be my friend, always give me rice to eat.
